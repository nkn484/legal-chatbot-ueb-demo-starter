# M08.1 LLM query planner — controlled benchmark evidence

## Implementation scope

This lane adds only deterministic, provider-neutral benchmark aggregation and an opt-in PostgreSQL
fixture harness. It does not call an LLM, SHINE SHOP, Zalo, or any network endpoint. Metric inputs
contain UUIDs, booleans, outcome codes, fallback codes, and durations only; they do not retain a
question, planner response, expansion query, source metadata, or citation text.

## Metric definitions

- **Hit@K**: macro fraction of non-empty-gold fixtures with at least one gold chunk in ranks `1..K`.
- **Recall@K**: macro average, over non-empty-gold fixtures, of retrieved gold chunks divided by gold
  chunks.
- **MRR**: macro average reciprocal rank of the first gold chunk, over non-empty-gold fixtures.
- **NO_RESULTS rate**: fraction of all fixtures whose retrieval decision is `NO_RESULTS`.
- **Wrong-scope rate**: fraction of all fixtures marked by the server-owned fixture oracle as containing
  evidence outside the expected legal scope.
- **Anchor-drift rate**: fraction of all fixtures marked by the protected-identity fixture oracle as
  drifting from its allowed anchor identity.
- **Planner latency p50/p95**: nearest-rank percentile (`ceil(p*n)-1`) over available planner durations.
- **End-to-end latency delta**: planned mean end-to-end duration minus raw mean end-to-end duration for
  exactly matched fixture IDs.
- **Fallback rates**: total, expected injected, and unexpected runtime fallback counts divided by all
  fixtures. Empty denominators and absent planner durations are reported as `0.0`.

The controlled fixture seeds natural-language strict lexical failure with planned recovery, an
original-top retention case, a no-match case, and a wrong-scope trap. It parses a valid anchor plan with
the real strict parser, resolves that anchor through the latest-ingested VBQPPL fixture metadata, and
rejects invented document, number, Article-Clause-Point, agency, time, and legal-status probes before
deriving the content-free anchor-drift metric. Its assertions enforce no lower overall
Hit@K/Recall@K/MRR, lower target NO_RESULTS, non-increasing wrong-scope rate, zero anchor drift,
original top-1 retention, and one retrieval run/citation chain per raw or planned case.
